Projectile Missions

Three short missions about gravity and objects moving sideways while they fall. Figure out the pattern in each one to move on.

The three missions (they unlock in order)

📦
1 · Warehouse Drop
Climb the shelves and drop packages onto robots rolling by below. The shelf height changes every time.
Clear: 5 catches in a row
🏴‍☠️
2 · Pirate Cannon
Your cannon only fires sideways. Pick its launch speed so the cannonball lands on the shark swimming out from under the ship.
Clear: 5 hits in a row
🏹
3 · Archer Duel
Aim at any angle, but your bow has a top speed. You and the computer swap between the tower and the ground every round.
Clear: win the best of 7

How it works

  • Each mission starts with a short briefing. After every attempt you see what happened and the numbers behind it.
  • The log under the scene keeps a record of your attempts. Look for patterns in it!
  • Finish a mission to earn its ★ and unlock the next one. Your progress is saved on this device.
  • After each mission you'll answer 3 short questions about what you discovered. You can't move on until they're answered completely, in your own words.
  • Finish all three missions to get your completion code. Your answers are turned in with it.
  • Tap 📖 How to play any time to see this again.

🔑 Teacher unlock
